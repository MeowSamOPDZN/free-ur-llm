how does this work?

FreeUr LLM has two brains. An intent brain understands greetings like "hi", "bro" and "how are you?" and reacts instantly. A neural brain — a tiny character-level transformer (~350K parameters) trained from scratch with PyTorch on 4,000+ chat conversations — generates replies for everything else. It knows every English word from a built-in dictionary (words.txt, 466,550 words). Everything runs locally in this page — no servers, no tracking. Code & training scripts are open source on GitHub.
